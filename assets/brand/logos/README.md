# PAI Systems — Logo kit v1

All logo SVGs have a transparent canvas and may be scaled without loss.

## Use

| Situation | File |
| --- | --- |
| Website / documents on a light background | `pai-systems-horizontal-ink.svg` |
| Website / video on a dark background | `pai-systems-horizontal-light.svg` |
| Hero, social and campaign pieces on light backgrounds | `pai-systems-horizontal-color.svg` |
| Narrow layouts, presentations and mobile covers | `pai-systems-stacked-ink.svg`, `pai-systems-stacked-light.svg` or `pai-systems-stacked-color.svg` |
| App, favicon and social avatar | `pai-systems-app-icon.svg` |
| Very small placements | `pai-systems-mark-*.svg` |

## Brand tokens

- Ink: `#12121A`
- Mineral white: `#EFEDE7`
- Signal green: `#B7FF3C`
- Violet: `#6A5CFF`

## Rules

Use the ink logo on mineral white and the light logo on ink. Keep clear space equal to the height of one green plane around every logo. Do not place the ink wordmark on a dark or photographic background. The preferred type style is Manrope: 700 for the wordmark and 300–400 for editorial display copy.
